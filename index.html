App

Every Shade of Life

I lead a weekly Bible study, and I wanted one place where my group could follow the lesson, pray, journal, and stay connected between meetings. So I designed and built it.

Each week's lesson connects to daily readings and prayers, with a private journal, a community board and prayer wall, group chat, class scheduling, and a leader dashboard. The name comes from the rainbow in Genesis 9, God's covenant promise, which opens the app every time. It's personal, too: God put a rainbow in the sky the day I was baptized, September 27, 2026.

a lesson I wrote

Put It On My Account

A 60-minute small group study on forgiveness, guilt, and the freedom Christ has already made available. It moves through Philemon, the parable of the unforgiving servant, and Peter and Judas, then into what psychology research shows about resentment and forgiveness.

Written to be safe for everyone in the room, with a gentle note that forgiveness never means returning to harm, and pointers to a pastor, counselor, or the 988 line.

Writing

Spirit Awakens

A short story in eight chapters about faith, love, and learning to live fully. A young woman meets a kindred spirit in her dreams and, with the help of a guide she has known since childhood, learns to be present in her waking life.

From Chapter One

The alarm cuts through my dream like a knife. Beep. Beep. Beep. 7:30 A.M. I drag myself out of bed and shuffle to the bathroom. My cat, Fate, follows behind me, eyes squinted against the morning light. She always does this, keeps watch, I think.

Today's my Family Communication class. We're doing presentations, and for some reason, I feel... confident? That's unusual for me. Normally, I'd be drowning in anxiety right now, heart racing with a familiar dread of speaking in front of people.

I keep talking, and as I do, I get more comfortable. By the end, I'm asking the class discussion questions, and people are actually engaging. It's funny, I feel warm inside. Proud, maybe, because for once I had not disappeared inside myself.

"Why do you think that is?" The question surfaces in my mind, but it's not mine. It's Yochanan's.

a note from the author

I wrote Spirit Awakens as a gift to someone I love, and it became a story that holds all the love, and all the struggles, of my life. It is shaped by other people's stories of love and loss, and by the songs, scripture, and quiet moments that spoke to me when I needed them most.

Above all, I thank my Lord and Savior Jesus Christ for this piece. He is the reason I believe in my dreams enough to create at all.

To everyone who has helped me live more fully: thank you. You are woven into every page.
